Binky vs Unclutter

Unclutter slides down from the top of the screen: clipboard, notes, and a small files shelf. Binky doesn't hide files in a drawer — it routes by type into destinations and holds unknowns in Review.

Choose Unclutter for quick stash-and-grab

When you want a temporary pile within reach and you're organizing by hand from there.

Choose Binky for always-on routing

When the Downloads folder (or any source) needs steady sorting, gatekeeping, and undo-friendly history.
